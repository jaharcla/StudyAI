Cool ledger

Evidence first: cool, exact, traceable. One shell, four surfaces, same content — so the three directions can be compared directly. Static mockup for a decision; not product code. No winner is declared.

Rationale — what this world says, suits and costs

Says
The workspace is an evidence ledger. Every answer is tagged observation or interpretation and tied back to a page, so trust is visible rather than promised.
Suits
Science courses where grounding, checks and confidence matter — physics, chemistry, maths — and students who audit what the tutor says.
Cost
Cool neutrals can read clinical or enterprise. It needs deliberate weight steps and the teal reserved strictly for state, or the hierarchy flattens into generic SaaS.
Reuse
The neutral base in `src/styles.css`, `.science-result`, `Citation.tsx`, `.context-card`, `.canvas-object`, the `coach.css` dock and its focus ring, and tabular numerals for measurements.
Motion
One authored moment: the evidence tag on a new tutor line fades in 160ms; nothing else moves.
Honest risk
Least expressive of the three. Warmth has to come from craft, not colour.

Forces in balance

An object is in equilibrium when the vector sum of the forces on it is zero. Resolving each force into horizontal and vertical components turns one diagram into two independent checks.

ΣFx = 0    ΣFy = 0
