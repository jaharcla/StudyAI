## Manual checklist — NOT performed (no stylus hardware in this environment)

These remain **unverified**. Each needs a real stylus on a touch device and a human observer:

- [ ] Pressure sensitivity: light vs firm strokes produce visibly thinner vs thicker ink from tip to tail.
- [ ] Velocity taper: a fast flick thins toward the ends; a slow stroke stays even.
- [ ] Palm rejection: resting a palm on glass while writing adds no strokes and does not stop the pen.
- [ ] Mouse users: with Palm rejection checked, mouse drawing works before and after any pen contact.
- [ ] Toggle persistence: uncheck Palm rejection, reload, touch drawing works; recheck, pen contact re-arms rejection.
- [ ] Scratch-out: crossing out a handwritten stroke removes exactly that stroke; one Undo restores it.
- [ ] Circle-select: a loop around two objects selects only those inside; nothing is erased.
- [ ] Double-tap: two taps on handwriting opens the LaTeX proposal; Dismiss leaves ink, objects and revision unchanged; Apply adds the reviewed equation.
- [ ] Latency/feel: no visible lag or jump between the nib and the ink during long strokes.

No hardware behaviour is claimed above the synthetic-pointer tests.
