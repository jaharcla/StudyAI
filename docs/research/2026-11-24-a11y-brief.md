# Accessibility and keyboard polish pass

Goal: make the shipped shell usable without a mouse and readable under the real themes, then fix what the browser shows. Not a theoretical audit.

## What to check and fix

1. Keyboard-only path: sidebar navigation, course/lesson selection, Tutor input and Coach Continue, canvas object selection and editor, steppable example controls, artifact open/download, progress dialog, settings. Every interactive control reachable by Tab with a visible focus ring and a logical order.
2. Contrast in light and dark themes: body text ≥4.5:1, large text and important graphics ≥3:1. Tint secondary text from the surface hue rather than neutral gray.
3. Hit targets ≥44px for repeated inline actions; no overlapping controls; no control hidden under the Tutor panel or Coach dock.
4. Semantics: buttons vs links, dialog roles and labels, `aria-live` for status, meaningful accessible names, no raw IDs in accessible names.
5. Reduced motion: docks, step rail and narration still convey state without animation.
